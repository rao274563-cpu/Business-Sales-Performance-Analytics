# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard built using the Online Retail dataset. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends across different countries.

The objective is to help businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products and customers, and analyze sales trends over time.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

* **Total Revenue** – Total sales generated across all transactions.
* **Total Orders** – Total number of orders placed.
* **Total Customers** – Number of unique customers.
* **Average Order Value (AOV)** – Average revenue generated per order.

---

## Dashboard Features

### Revenue by Country

* Displays revenue generated by each country.
* Helps identify high-performing markets.

### Monthly Revenue Trend

* Visualizes revenue changes over time.
* Highlights seasonal patterns and growth trends.

### Top Customers

* Shows customers contributing the highest revenue.
* Useful for customer segmentation and retention strategies.

### Top Products

* Identifies best-selling products based on revenue.
* Helps understand product demand and performance.

### Country Sales Heatmap

* Displays monthly sales intensity across countries.
* Enables quick identification of regional sales patterns.

### Interactive Filters

* Country Filter
* Invoice Date Filter

All visualizations update dynamically based on selected filters.

---

## Key Insights

* United Kingdom generated the highest revenue among all countries.
* Revenue showed strong growth during the later months of the dataset period.
* A small group of customers contributed a significant share of total sales.
* Several products consistently outperformed others in sales revenue.
* Sales performance varied significantly across countries and months.
